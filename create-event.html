The basics

Mode

Sign-ups and fee

Podium does not take sign-ups or money. Keep that on your own form, where it already works, and paste the link here so teams can find it.

Deck rules

Every event takes PDF or PPTX, up to 25 MB. One deck per team.

Voting

Comma separated. Each person gets one vote in every group. Leave it blank and they get one vote for the whole event.

Who can vote

Counts stay hidden from everyone until you close voting. Teams cannot vote for themselves.

Who can find it

Listed events show up in the open events list, so people can wander in and vote.

You can change all of this until voting opens.
