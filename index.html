A few things worth knowing.

Voting, money, and what happens to your slides later.

Who can vote?

Anyone signed in who has filled in their profile. Back as many decks as you like, but only once each, and a vote cannot be taken back — the button turns into "You have voted" and closes. You cannot vote for your own team.

Can I see the counts while voting is open?

No. Counts stay hidden until the organiser ends voting. That keeps the race fair and stops an early leader from running away with it.

How do I enter the contest?

Sign up on the poster. The poster carries the organiser's Google Form and payment QR, and the organiser checks the proof there. Podium starts after that: you come here with the event code, upload your deck, and the room votes.

What can I upload, and what happens to it?

PDF or PPTX, up to 25 MB, and up to 15 slides unless the organiser sets another limit. PDF is safer: your fonts and layout stay exactly as you made them. Your slides then show up as pictures with your team name on them, and the file itself stays private unless the organiser turns downloads on. The deck is open to everyone for 30 days, then only you and the organiser can see it, and we delete the file after 90 days. Winning decks stay on the results page, and you can delete yours any time.

How do I become an organiser?

Someone running the fest sends you an invite link. Sign in first, then open the link. Your account turns into an organiser account and you can make the event.
